How it works, and how to check it

  
Every number comes from one equation, Δ/SE = za + zb, under a normal approximation. The left side is the budget — how many standard errors the smallest effect worth detecting spans; the right side is how it is spent, on protection against false alarms and against misses. Sample sizes are always rounded up. The math is small enough to read in ten minutes (src/core/), and a reference script (reference/two_condition.py) prints every number the acceptance tests pin down.

  
WhyN covers one design family: a two-condition comparison — rates or means, paired or independent, a main effect or an interaction. For designs it does not cover (ANOVA, regression, survival, cluster-randomized, sequential or Bayesian), use G*Power, Superpower or DeclareDesign instead.
